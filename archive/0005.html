News Troll · week of 13 September 2026

Issue 5.

This issue keeps running into the same argument from different directions: that claims of intelligence, classicism, or authenticity dissolve under scrutiny, and what's left is craft, dependency, and the people who actually do the work. From a South African tech columnist picking apart AGI marketing to a menswear tastemaker who dresses without checking a mirror, the throughline is a distrust of anyone selling certainty. Elsewhere, an AI reads cities through rival theories, a novelist lets a machine narrate late capitalism, and a critic defends the humble concert program note against its own extinction.
