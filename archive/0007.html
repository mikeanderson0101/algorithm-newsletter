Issue 7.

This issue keeps finding the same argument in different rooms: that whatever refuses to be smoothed into a category — outsider art, a shapeshifting novel, an underappreciated Chicago poet, a closeted-then-defiant matinee idol — outlasts whatever tried to flatten it. Even the pieces about AI turn out to be about the same thing, asking what creativity and cities look like once a machine has been trained to prefer the average.
