Refresh Song TrollSong Troll refreshes on its own, whenever you want, separately from News Troll. Each refresh adds 10 songs, picked from an archive of music sites’ weekly lists back to 2025. A few cents a batch.

  1. 01

    Press the button

    It copies your thumbs and opens the refresh on GitHub, in a new tab.

  2. 02

    Paste and run

    Click Run workflow. Choose which years to pick from (all, or a single year like 2026). Paste into the taste box so the batch leans your way; your thumbs are kept from then on. Then click the green Run workflow button.

  3. 03

    Come back in a few minutes

    The new batch appears here at the top, tagged New.
