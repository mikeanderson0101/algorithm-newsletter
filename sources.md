# Troller — Source List

Last verified: 24 October 2026, by fetching every feed and counting items from the last 14 days.

This file mirrors `assets/feeds.json`, which is what `scripts/weekly.js` actually reads. Change the JSON, then this.

## Rules

1. **New writing about new things.** A piece runs only if it is mainly about new work from roughly the last three months: a release, show, building, book, comic, collection, label, typeface. Older subjects only when pegged to something new (a reissue, restoration, new edition, new exhibition). Both judges check this.
2. **English only**, published in English by the outlet. Never translated.
3. **Section feeds over whole-site feeds.** A whole-site feed lets news, shopping and sponsored posts crowd out criticism. Use an outlet's reviews or criticism feed wherever one exists.
4. **essay** feeds are mostly criticism and long-form. **news** feeds are mixed: they are listed after essays when shortlisting and judged on the article, never the masthead.
5. **Rotation:** an outlet holds a section in at most 2 of any 4 weekly issues. Feeds from one outlet, such as the Guardian's, count as one outlet.

## Dropped on 24 Oct 2026, and why

- **Filmmaker Magazine**: the feed now serves casino SEO spam.
- **Egyptian Streets, Open Magazine, Esquire India**: whole-site feeds that put football, cricket, exchange rates and phone deals into Film and Fashion.
- **Yanko Design, Design Milk, ArchDaily, Archinect**: product and project showcases, firm news and buy links rather than criticism.
- **i-D, Fashion Journal, W Magazine, Glossy, Opumo, Mr Porter Journal**: off-subject interviews, celebrity styling, business news, shopping, or untitled items.
- **Well Dressed Dad, He Spoke Style, From Squalor to Baller, Menswear Musings, Hodinkee**: shopping guides and watch reviews.
- **Aotearoa NZ Review of Books**: the feed address now serves unrelated SEO posts.
- **Aeon** (under Tech): excellent essays, rarely about technology; every one was rejected as off-subject.
- **Talkhouse, Screen Anarchy, Consequence, The AU Review, Rolling Stone MENA, Unorthodox Reviews, The Line of Best Fit**: news, gig reports and announcements.
- **Clarkesworld, Literalab, B O D Y, PREE, sx salon and other general literary journals**: fiction rather than criticism, or no recent items; Books now leans SFF and comics.
